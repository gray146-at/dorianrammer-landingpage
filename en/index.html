Sports Betting Research

Quotenlabor

Research, not gut feeling.

Quotenlabor is emerging from my ongoing research into sports betting, prediction markets and probabilistic decision-making.

The focus is on statistical models, bookmaker odds, market prices and probability estimation - and on the question of when a quoted price actually diverges from its mathematically expected value. This includes Kelly benchmarking, Poisson models, market inefficiencies, value betting and Expected Value (EV).

Planned
  • A look behind the models and research process
  • Analysis and benchmarking of sports betting markets

  • A Telegram service featuring selected analyses and signals
  • Proprietary tools and research workflows
