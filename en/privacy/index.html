Privacy

This privacy policy applies to dorianrammer.at and to the blog Widerstandskraft (widerstandskraft.link). The two websites use different services; what applies to which is stated in each section. The Alexander Forum has its own privacy policy. The German version is authoritative.

dorianrammer.at

Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver and secure the pages. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts are served by this website itself, not by third-party servers.

Statistics. Simple Analytics (Simple Analytics B.V., Netherlands) is used for visitor statistics. It sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses; only aggregated figures such as pages viewed and referrers are recorded. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in knowing which content is read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Email. If you write to me, I process your address and message in order to reply and keep them for as long as the conversation requires.

Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and other platforms are plain links. Only when you click them do you go to the respective provider.

Widerstandskraft

Platform and hosting. Widerstandskraft runs on Ghost(Pro) by the Ghost Foundation. When you visit, technically necessary data such as your IP address is processed in order to deliver and secure the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Images, fonts (Bunny Fonts, Slovenia, no cookies) and scripts for search and sign-up (jsDelivr) are loaded from content delivery networks, which receive your IP address. See Ghost’s privacy policy.

Newsletter and membership. When you sign up, your email address, any name you provide, the time of sign-up and your newsletter settings are stored. Newsletters are sent via Ghost. Ghost may record whether a newsletter was opened and which links in it were clicked. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email or in your account. Signed-in members receive a cookie so that they stay logged in.

Supporting membership. Paid memberships are processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland). You enter payment details directly with Stripe; I only see that and for what a payment was made. The legal basis is the performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Comments. Members can comment on posts. Name and comment are publicly visible.

Statistics. Simple Analytics as on dorianrammer.at, without cookies and without IP addresses.

Microsoft Clarity. Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.) is currently also embedded. It analyses how pages are used (such as clicks and scrolling), sets cookies for this and transfers data to Microsoft. Whether Clarity stays is being decided; this policy will be updated accordingly.

Share buttons. A script from ShareThis (USA) is loaded so that posts can be shared. Data such as your IP address is transferred to ShareThis, which may set cookies.

Ko-fi. A Ko-fi button (Ko-fi Labs Ltd., United Kingdom) is embedded on some pages. When it loads, your IP address is transferred to Ko-fi. If you support via Ko-fi, its privacy policy applies.

Links to social networks and videos (such as YouTube) are plain links, not embedded content.
