Press material

Short bio

Dorian Rammer (born 1996) is a writer and public commentator based in Vienna. He writes essays on mental health, addiction and resilience, speaks publicly about addiction and stigma, and in 2025 founded the Alexander Forum für Reflexion & Resilienz, an independent think & do tank he directs.

Longer bio

Dorian Rammer, born in 1996, is a writer and public commentator based in Vienna. His career has taken him through emergency medical services, local politics, marketing and three years of training in early childhood education. From 2020 to the end of 2021 he served as a district councillor in Vienna’s Mariahilf district and then returned to activism; in summer 2025 he left the Social Democratic Party (SPÖ). Since 2023 he has campaigned for addiction prevention and mental health, especially in the LGBTIQ community, and speaks openly about addiction, trauma and stigma, including in the 2025 ORF documentary “Thema Spezial: Sex auf Drogen”. He publishes essays at widerstandskraft.link, articles and short videos, and directs the Alexander Forum für Reflexion & Resilienz, which he founded in 2025.
